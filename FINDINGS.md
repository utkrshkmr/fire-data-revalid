# Findings — California fire sources vs FireSET (run of 2026-10-13)

586 FireSET fires (2018–2025) were checked against 13 agency sources and 3 satellite products. Every number below
comes from `fire-source-audit/out/` and is regenerated by `scripts/summarize_findings.py`.

## 1. The catalog was wrong in ways that changed the answers

Each of these was fixed before the run. Without the fixes the audit would have reported errors that are not real.

| Source | Problem found against live data | Effect if left alone |
|---|---|---|
| WFIGS (IRWIN) | The config compared `InitialLatitude/Longitude`. FireSET uses the feature geometry (IRWIN's current point of origin): it matches the geometry on 478 of 479 fires. The first-report point differs from it by >500 m on 193 fires and by >5 km on 56. | FireSET would "disagree" with its own source on a third of fires |
| InFORM | No `IrwinID` or text cause fields (`RelatedIrwinIDs`, coded `FireCauseID`). `FinalAcres` is null for every California record. California 2018–19 records are FireStat/WFMI back-fill, not IRWIN. | No ID matches, no cause, no size, wrong lineage |
| CAL FIRE incidents | API moved to `incidents.fire.ca.gov`; needs `&year=` (otherwise current season only); `Started` is UTC, not Pacific; 294 records carry a bulk 2019-01-04 "extinguished" date. | Source unreachable (HTTP 403) |
| FPA-FOD 7th ed. | No `IRWIN_ID`/`UNIQUE_FIRE_ID`. Records are copies of other systems (`SOURCE_SYSTEM`): 2020 = IA-IRWIN, 2021–24 = IA-INFORM. | Counted as independent of FireSET when it is the same IRWIN record |
| MTBS points | `BurnBndLat/Lon` is the burned-area centroid (Camp: 10 km from the origin). The reported origin is in `Event_ID`. | Every large fire flagged as a major disagreement |
| FRAP, FPA-FOD, MTBS dates | Calendar dates stored as 00:00 UTC were shifted into the previous Pacific day. | Every alarm date one day early |
| CAL FIRE FIRIS view | Holds only the current season (2026). | False same-name matches; now disabled |

The project also matched same-name fires from other seasons (21 cases, e.g. CAL FIRE "Peak" 2024 for Peak 2023).
Fuzzy matches are now rejected beyond 15 days.

## 2. How independent are the sources? Mostly they are not.

Points copied between systems were detected per fire (within 5 m, or an exact 3–4-decimal rounding) and merged
into one lineage before any consensus. Copies were merged on 364 fires.

| Pair | Fires with the identical point |
|---|---|
| FireSET = WFIGS | 573 |
| InFORM = WFIGS (same IRWIN record) | 396 |
| FPA-FOD = InFORM / WFIGS | 327 / 293 |
| MTBS `Event_ID` = WFIGS | 201 |
| USFS FireStat = WFIGS | 180 |
| CAL FIRE incident = WFIGS | 59 |

After merging, independent reporting lineages per fire:

| Lineages | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fires | 4 | 195 | 335 | 49 | 3 |

So **for a third of the fires there is no second, independent agency location at all**. Where there is one, it is
almost always the CAL FIRE incident record or a FireStat/state record from 2018–19. Answer to the open question in
the brief: FPA-FOD, InFORM and FireStat are *not* independent of IRWIN for 2020 onward, and MTBS's point is a
rounded copy of whichever agency point it was given.

Perimeters behave the same way: FRAP, WFIGS, USFS EDW and NIFC history are usually one polygon passed between
systems (IoU ≥ 0.999 on 69–94% of shared fires). MTBS, mapped from Landsat, is the only independent perimeter. It
agrees with the agency polygon at a median IoU of 0.94, and its area is a median 2% **larger**, not smaller as the
catalog expected.

## 3. Ignition points

Agreement across independent lineages: agree (≤500 m) 124 fires, minor (≤2 km) 154, major (>2 km) 109,
single lineage 195, none 4.

Distance from each source's point to the other lineages' consensus:

| Source | n | Median | 90th pct | >2 km | >375 m outside final perimeter |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| USFS FireStat | 139 | 719 m | 4.0 km | 13% | 9% |
| InFORM | 348 | 748 m | 4.6 km | 15% | 17% |
| WFIGS / IRWIN | 363 | 793 m | 4.6 km | 16% | 23% |
| CAL FIRE incidents | 340 | 805 m | 5.0 km | 21% | 28% |
| FPA-FOD | 280 | 907 m | 4.8 km | 17% | 18% |
| MTBS (`Event_ID`) | 143 | 1.3 km | 5.7 km | 25% | 22% |

Typical agency-to-agency disagreement is about 800 m, and roughly 1 fire in 6 differs by more than 2 km. 5% of
WFIGS points carry only 2 decimals (±~550 m). 24% of FireSET points sit more than 375 m outside the final perimeter.

## 4. FireSET ignition corrections (`out/fireset_ignition_patch.csv`)

FireSET vs the recommended point: <100 m 435 fires, 100–500 m 24, 0.5–1 km 29, 1–2 km 37, 2–5 km 34, >5 km 21.

127 fires are listed (moves over 500 m, or FireSET has no point). Each row carries an `action`:

- **replace — 62 fires.** 50 where FireSET's point lies outside the final perimeter and the new agency point is
  inside it, 8 where every reporting lineage agrees on the new point, and 4 where FireSET has no point. Median move
  1.5 km. New points come from CAL FIRE incidents (31), InFORM final-report points (25) and FPA-FOD (6).
- **review — 65 fires.** Both points plausible (often both inside the perimeter), or only a satellite fallback
  exists (4 fires, including 5-6 Cascade Creek 2020 whose FireSET point is 30 km from its perimeter and no agency
  record matched).

Examples: Sites 2024 (FireSET 6.4 km outside the perimeter; InFORM's final report for the same IRWIN fire and CAL
FIRE agree on a point inside it), O'Neals 2018 (FireSET is a 2-decimal point 7.7 km away; CAL FIRE and the state
record agree within 17 m). CAL FIRE incident coordinates are map locations, not investigated origins; review rows
where they are the only support before applying.

## 5. Discovery times

IRWIN, CAL FIRE and state records agree (median offset ≈ 0). GOES-R's first detection lands a median 0.17 h after
the reported discovery (within 1 h on 275 of 391 fires), which independently confirms FireSET's `Start_Local`.

Systematic clock errors, attributed with the satellite's first heat as referee:

- **USFS FireStat**: its local clock runs 2 h ahead of Pacific in 2018–19 (Delta 2018: 14:51 vs the real 12:51
  PDT). This error is inherited by InFORM back-fill and FPA-FOD (+2 h). The EDW service then tags those values as
  UTC (−5 h), and in 2020 it serves Pacific local time as UTC (−7 h). 67 EDW records, 2018–2020; correct from 2023.
- CAL FIRE incidents: 26 records with whole-hour offsets, 11 of them in 2020.
- FireSET itself: 4 fires.

First VIIRS detection inside the perimeter is more than 3 h before the reported discovery on 37 fires (holdovers,
late discovery, or heat from a neighbouring fire inside the perimeter).

## 6. Size and cause

Reported final sizes agree within 1% (median) because they are mostly the same polygon's acreage. 114 fires have
a reported size more than 25% off; the main cause is IRWIN incident sizes never updated past discovery (36 fires
whose WFIGS size is 0.01–0.3 acres against hundreds or thousands mapped), carried into InFORM and FPA-FOD. WFIGS
perimeters' `poly_GISAcres` is a placeholder (0.1, 1, 20 ac) on many 2020 polygons; the run uses
`poly_Acres_AutoCalc`.
Cause classes rarely conflict: 3 human-vs-natural conflicts among 570 fires with two or more causes. Consensus is
Undetermined 279, Natural 179, Human 128.

## 7. Limits

- Recommended points are agency records scored on support, perimeter, satellite proximity, trust and precision. The
  scoring favours points inside the final perimeter, which is right for an origin but not proof of one.
- The GOES search is centred on FireSET's point (5 km radius), so it can confirm timing but is not an independent
  location check.
- FireSET's own VIIRS `.h5` files were not on this machine; the FIRMS S-NPP and NOAA-20 archives were used instead
  (no NOAA-21).
- NASA FEDS, InciWeb and NIROPS were not used (no archive endpoint / manual sources).
